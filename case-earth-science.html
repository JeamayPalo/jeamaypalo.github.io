Case study

Turning Earth’s Vital Signs into Stories

JPL Earth Science Presentation Library

  • Presentations
  • Multimedia
  • Copywriting

Challenge

JPL’s Earth-observing satellites take our planet’s vital signs from orbit. They measure sea level, groundwater, soil moisture, plant health, and carbon in the atmosphere, and that data helps track climate change, forecast weather and floods, and manage water and food for people around the world. But data from orbit doesn’t explain itself. Each mission needed a story that Congress, leaders, and the public could follow.

My Solution

I built a library of mission decks for SMAP, GRACE and GRACE-FO, ECOSTRESS, the Orbiting Carbon Observatories, and the TOPEX/Jason series with SWOT. In each one I paired JPL’s video animations with visuals from NASA’s Eyes on the Earth and Eyes on the Solar System, then wrote short, clear copy so the science came through in seconds. I gave every deck the same look, so the missions read as one story.

Impact

The library of visually engaging mission stories is now published on JPL’s Earth Science site, where anyone can download and present it.
