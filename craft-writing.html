How I work on multi-author documents

  • Gather input across departments

    I collect and reconcile input from science, policy and program teams, an average of more than 10 contributors per document.

  • Hold fixed deadlines

    Conference papers and annual reports run on fixed submission and publication dates. I chase inputs from co-authors and contributors to meet them.

  • Draft the text

    I draft most of the text myself, then edit contributor input so the finished document reads as one.
