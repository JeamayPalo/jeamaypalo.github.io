Case study

Keeping America in Orbit

ISS Program and Commercial LEO Program · NASA Headquarters

  • Communication Strategy
  • Congressional Hearings
  • Executive Briefings
  • One-Pagers

Challenge

The International Space Station was at a turning point. Its 2028 retirement was approaching, and NASA had to plan a handoff to the Commercial Low-Earth Orbit program ensuring there would be no gap in America’s presence there. Congress and other key stakeholders needed a clear reason to invest in what came next.

My Solution

I led communication strategy for the ISS program at NASA Headquarters. I took dense program, budget, and transition plans and turned them into briefing materials that decision-makers could absorb quickly, including materials for Congressional hearings. I also wrote executive briefings and one-pagers that gave senior NASA leaders one clear, consistent story to tell.

Impact

My briefing materials supported the case that extended the ISS to 2030 and helped shape NASA’s Commercial LEO strategy.

I produced 24+ briefing presentations delivered to 2,000+ audience members at national and international conferences.
