Case study

Telling the Story of Digital Transformation and Service Delivery

U.S. Customs and Border Protection, Digital Transformation Office

  • Annual & Quarterly Reports
  • Executive Briefings
  • UX Research
  • Usability Testing
  • Web Modernization
  • Multimedia Video

Challenge

At CBP, digital transformation is where mission meets service delivery. The agency is modernizing the digital tools and experiences its employees rely on and its public customers use every day. But modernization only works when people understand it. Leaders need to see the impact, employees need to see what’s changing for them, and the public needs to know how it serves them better.

My Solution

I translate CBP’s modernization work into stories that each audience can follow. I design executive briefings that show leaders progress and impact. I support user research, usability testing, and web modernization efforts that streamline cbp.gov content, and I produce creative multimedia videos that explain new tools in plain terms. I also write the Digital Transformation Office’s annual and quarterly reports, which capture its performance across the agency.

Impact

A public annual report on cbp.gov, a streamlined cbp.gov experience, and briefings, videos, and reports that make CBP’s modernization visible to leaders, employees, and the public.
